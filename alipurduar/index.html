What makes Alipurduar worth visiting

  • Jaldapara National Park, one of the last strongholds of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros
  • Buxa Tiger Reserve, straddling the forested hills toward Bhutan
  • the sprawling tea estates of the Dooars, many open for guided garden walks
